ABOUT MINGLU ZHONG

钟明璐（Minglu Zhong）毕业于艺术中心设计学院（ArtCenter College of Design）插画专业（BFA）与马里兰艺术学院（Maryland Institute College of Art）策展实践专业（MFA）。她的实践融合视觉艺术、设计原则与以人为本的文化视野，策展方向聚焦于当代艺术与全球文化的交汇，支持跨学科艺术家创造超越传统边界的沉浸式体验。她借助传统与数字平台，营造鼓励跨文化对话与哲学思考的互动学习体验，同时倡导公平与多元。

她曾在巴尔的摩策展了群展 Your Pixel Echo（2024）与 Just a Drop（2023）；并与约翰霍普金斯大学、陶森大学合作组织了 From Niche to Mainstream: the Sacralization of Comic-Con Culture（2023）与 Inter-Asian Cultural Expo（2023）等活动。目前担任洛杉矶 4C Gallery 策展与设计助理（Curatorial and Design Assistant），以及纽约 RainRain Gallery 策展助理（Curatorial Assistant）。目前常驻纽约。

原文 / Original

Holding a BFA in Illustration from ArtCenter College of Design and an MFA in Curatorial Practice from Maryland Institute College of Art, Minglu’s work blends visual art, design principles, and human-centered cultures. Her curatorial approach focuses on exploring the intersections of contemporary art and global culture, supporting interdisciplinary artists in creating immersive experiences that transcend conventional boundaries. By leveraging traditional and digital platforms, she fosters interactive learning experiences that provoke intercultural dialogue and philosophical reflection while promoting equity and diversity.

She has curated exhibitions such as Your Pixel Echo (2024) and Just a Drop (2023) in Baltimore. She has organized events in collaboration with Johns Hopkins University and Towson University, including From Niche to Mainstream: the Sacralization of Comic-Con Culture (2023) and Inter-Asian Cultural Expo (2023). Currently, she is the Curatorial and Design Assistant at 4C Gallery (Los Angeles) and the Curatorial Assistant at RainRain Gallery (New York City). She is currently based in New York.
